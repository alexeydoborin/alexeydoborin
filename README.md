## 🚀 Currently Building

**[Arvexo](https://arvexo.ru)** is an independent AI studio: products, research and consulting. Two main products right now:

### 🏆 Arvexo Olympiad Arena
Educational and competitive platform for school students and early-career specialists. It teaches olympiad-level problem solving, research labs and projects, and turns results into a **verified portfolio**, where skills are proven by solved tasks, labs, code and tournaments instead of being self-declared.
- Course tracks, tasks and tournaments; long-term: Labs, team projects, portfolio and opportunities
- SSO through Arvexo Account (authorization-code flow)
- **Stack:** FastAPI, SQLAlchemy 2, Alembic, PostgreSQL, Next.js 16, React 19, Tailwind 4, Web Push

### 📡 Arvexo Radar
Enterprise AI Effectiveness & Knowledge Platform. It answers an IT director's questions: how employees use AI, whether AI infrastructure pays off, and which practices to scale.
- **Visibility:** usage, models, agents, tokens, cost, latency and errors from content-free telemetry (hashed user IDs, no prompt texts stored)
- **Business value:** full AI TCO, FTE saved, net benefit, ROI and payback
- **Knowledge sharing:** best-practice detection, expert review, publication and adoption tracking
- **Stack:** FastAPI, async SQLAlchemy, PostgreSQL + pgvector, Alembic, Next.js 15, TypeScript, Tailwind

Also part of the ecosystem: [Consulting](https://ai.arvexo.ru), Account (SSO), Connect (smart VPN), Ai-shop (Telegram store).
